### What the competition is

In the YIS Global Stock Pitch Competition, you pick one publicly traded company, research it the way a professional analyst would, and make a recommendation: should investors buy the stock or sell it? Then you back that call up with evidence. You can enter on your own or with one partner. You don't need any experience with investing to start. This site walks you through every step.

### What you submit

You turn in three things:

1. **A written report.** A PDF of no more than {limits.reportMaxPages} pages. You can add an appendix of any length for extra charts and tables.
2. **A slide deck.** A PowerPoint or PDF file that summarizes your report. Keynote files aren't accepted, so if you use Google Slides, download it as a PowerPoint.
3. **A video.** A YouTube link to a video of you presenting your pitch with your slides on screen. It must be under {limits.videoMaxMinutes} minutes, and you can't speed it up. You don't need to edit it.

All three go in one email to <a href={`mailto:${submission.email}`}>{submission.email}</a> by {deadline.display}. That's Pacific time, which means {deadlineEastern} here in North Carolina. Don't plan around that, though. GHIC's target is {target}, so a bad internet night or a broken file never costs you your entry.

### How you're scored

Judges score every entry out of {rubricTotalPoints}:

- **Investment Idea: {categoryPoints('investment-idea')} points.** Does your recommendation make sense, show real insight into the company and its industry, and rest on sound financial analysis? This also covers your <GlossaryTerm id="esg">ESG</GlossaryTerm> and AI sections.
- **Presentation: {categoryPoints('presentation')} points.** Do your slides use clear bullets that you explain, and does your pitch keep a good pace?
- **YouTube Video: {categoryPoints('video')} points.** Do you present clearly, stay under the time limit, know your company, and come across as professional?

The full breakdown is on the [Rules page](/rules#rubric). The big lesson: a great report with a weak video can lose to a good report with a strong one. Plan to spend real time practicing your pitch.

### Rules that can get you disqualified

These are the rules that matter most. Break one and your entry may not be scored at all.

- **It has to be your own work.** Paying or hiring anyone to research, write, or finish any part of your entry is banned.
- **AI can help you learn, not write.** You can use AI to explain a concept or point you toward research, but you can't use it to write any part of your report, slides, or analysis. Check anything AI tells you against a real source.
- **Use only public information.** Everything in your pitch has to come from sources anyone could find.
- **Attach your files.** Google Drive links aren't accepted, and judges must be able to open everything without asking for permission.
- **Be ready to defend it.** You need to be able to explain every number and every claim you make.

### How GHIC gets you there

GHIC breaks the work into small steps with checkpoints, so you're never facing the whole thing at once. The [learning path](/learn) shows every module and its due date. Mentored teams also check in with an officer at least every two weeks and get feedback on each draft. Mentors ask questions and point out gaps, but they never write anything for you. That's both GHIC's rule and YIS's.
