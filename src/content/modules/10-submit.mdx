<Part id="why">
  Two months of work comes down to one email. YIS accepts no late submissions, and a missing attachment, a link judges can't open, or a file that needs permission can mean your entry isn't reviewed at all.

  This module is short on purpose: it's a checklist. Follow it exactly, submit early, and you're done.
</Part>

<Part id="learn">
  ### One email, three items

  Everything goes in one email to {email} by {deadline.display}:

  1. **Your report** as a PDF attachment, named <code class="[overflow-wrap:anywhere]">{reportFileName}</code>
  2. **Your slides** as a PowerPoint or PDF attachment, named <code class="[overflow-wrap:anywhere]">{slidesFileName}</code>
  3. **Your YouTube link**, pasted into the email

  GHIC's target is {target}, two days early, so a problem never costs you your entry.

  ### Attachments, not links

  YIS doesn't accept Google Drive links, and it won't review files that need permission to open. Attach the actual files.

  Watch out for large files. Gmail turns attachments over 25 MB into Drive links automatically, which YIS won't accept. If your slides are too big, export them as a PDF, which is allowed and usually much smaller, or compress the images in your deck.

  ### What goes in the email body

  - Each student's name
  - Each student's email address
  - Your school
  - Your state
  - Your YouTube link

  Copy your partner on the email.

  ### Before you hit send

  - **Registration:** every teammate must be registered on {registration.via} by {regCloses}.
  - **Open each attachment** from the email draft itself to check it's the right, final version.
  - **Test the YouTube link** in a private, signed-out browser window.
  - **Double-check the address:** {email}.

  ### After you send

  Check your Sent folder to confirm the email went out with both attachments. Forward a copy to your mentor, then tick off your last module. You're done.
</Part>

<Part id="do">
  1. **Confirm every teammate is registered** on {registration.via}.
  2. **Gather your final files:** the report PDF and the slides, correctly named.
  3. **Check the slides' file size.** If it's over 25 MB, export them as a PDF instead.
  4. **Write the email** using the example above, and copy your partner.
  5. **Open each attachment from the draft** to confirm it's the final version.
  6. **Test the YouTube link** in a private, signed-out window.
  7. **Send it by {target}.**
  8. **Check your Sent folder**, forward a copy to your mentor, and tick off module 10 in your [progress tracker](/learn#progress).

  **Deliverable:** your submission, sent. Due by <time datetime={new Date(frontmatter.due).toISOString().slice(0, 10)}>{formatDue(new Date(frontmatter.due))}</time>, but aim for {target}.
</Part>
